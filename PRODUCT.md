# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (existing codebase). Preview via `node serve.mjs`.

## Users

Mixed audience, all evaluating a digital signage platform for physical spaces in Türkiye:
- Retail chains' marketing and store-operations leads who run screens across many stores.
- Venue operators (malls, restaurants, hotels, pharmacies, offices) who run screens in one or a few locations.
- Brands and agencies who want to buy in-store screen time (retail media).

Their job on this page: understand what Mlink Signage does, believe it is easy to run and can earn money, and log in.

## Product Purpose

Mlink Signage is Mimeda's digital signage product: manage every in-store / in-venue screen from one panel, schedule and target content, monitor screen health, and report what played. Success = visitors who log in at https://staging.bonmlink.com.

## Positioning

Two confirmed differentiators:
1. **Retail media revenue** — the screen network becomes sellable ad inventory for brands, backed by Mimeda's retail-media business.
2. **Easy management** — one panel, fast setup, works on existing screens.

## Operating Context

Screens live in stores, aisles, checkout zones, mall corridors, restaurant counters and offices. Content changes by time of day, campaign dates and location.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Copy language: English.
- Feature claims on the current page (supported OSes, offline mode, proof-of-play, pricing model) are **unconfirmed** and must be verified by the user before launch.
- No form or demo CTA; every CTA is "Login" → https://staging.bonmlink.com.

## Brand Commitments

- Part of Mimeda (mimeda.com.tr). **Mimeda palette is binding**: blue `#009FE3`, orange `#FF6600` / `#F06C22`, ink `#414042`, light `#F4F4F4`.
- Typeface is free (Outfit is Mimeda's face but not required).
- Product name: **Mlink Signage** (formerly `bonmlink`). Header/footer show the Mimeda logo next to the product name.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No real metrics, customer names, logos or testimonials. Any number shown must be clearly labeled as example data; do not invent customers or benchmarks.

## Product Principles

1. Show the screens working, don't describe them.
2. Two promises only: it earns money, and it is easy to run.
3. No fabricated proof.
4. Speak to operators in their own vocabulary (store, aisle, checkout, campaign), not tech jargon.
