Frequently asked questions

Does it work with my existing screens?

Mlink Signage runs directly on most smart screens. For other screens, we add a small player connected over HDMI.

How do ad sales work?

You decide which screens are open to ads, and when. Those hours are offered to brands through Mimeda, and every play is reported.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Content is stored on the screen, so playback continues. New content downloads as soon as the connection is back.

How is pricing set?

A monthly license based on the number of screens. We prepare an offer tailored to your network.
